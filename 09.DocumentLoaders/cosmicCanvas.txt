The Cosmic Canvas
A silent breath in the deepest void,
Where space and time were first deployed.
From a singular point of burning light,
Burst forth the dawn, consuming night.

Great webs of gas and starry dust,
In gravity’s cold and ancient trust,
Spun into spirals, grand and wide,
Where newborn suns began to ride.

Black holes that churn in the quiet deep,
While weary planets spin and sleep.
A billion galaxies softly glow,
In an ocean of dark we barely know.

Through expanding realms of ice and fire,
The cosmic engine refuses to tire.
Supernovas flare and softly fade,
Leaving the heavy elements they made.

And here on a speck of vibrant blue,
We gaze at the endless, sprawling hue.
Forged from the ash of the stars above,
Seeking to map the expanse we love.
